Nothing posts without approval

Clipper sends a clip only after its owner ticks it, and only to accounts its owner has connected. On TikTok it arrives as a draft.

Post: tick each account, then Save

Untick to take a clip out again

Clips from watched channels wait too

Picks the moments worth clipping

The transcript goes to the AI service its owner chose, beside chat bursts, loudness jumps and YouTube's most replayed parts. Four frames of each clip then catch cutscenes and menus.

Clipper posts approved clips on a schedule. To YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

From 9 am to 11 pm by default, 2 hours apart, at most 6 a day for each account. TikTok gets drafts.

Questions about Clipper

Can I sign up for Clipper?

No. It runs on its owner's computer and is not offered to the public.

How does Clipper post to TikTok?

As a draft in the TikTok inbox, which the owner publishes in the app.

Where does Clipper keep tokens and clips?

On its owner's computer, in a file only they can read. No servers, no analytics.

How do I remove Clipper's access?

Disconnect it in Clipper, or in the platform's own settings.
