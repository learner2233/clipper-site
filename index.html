Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool that turns long gaming videos and streams into short vertical clips with captions. It runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

When its owner approves a clip, Clipper can send it to the owner's own accounts: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, a Facebook Page, and TikTok, where it arrives as a draft in the TikTok inbox for the owner to review and post in the TikTok app.

Clipper only ever posts to accounts its owner has connected, and only clips its owner has approved.
