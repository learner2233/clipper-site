Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

What Clipper receives

When you connect an account, Clipper receives an access token from that platform and your account's display name. For TikTok it uses the user.info.basic scope (your display name) and the video.upload scope (to send approved clips to your drafts).

Where it is kept

Tokens, clips and settings are stored only on the computer where Clipper runs, in a file only its owner can read. Clipper has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and does not sell or share your data.

How it is used

Your tokens are used only to send the clips you approve to your own accounts, and to show which account is connected. To pick moments for clips, the spoken words of a video are sent to the AI service its owner has chosen; no account data is included.

Removing your data

Disconnect the account in Clipper to delete its token, or remove Clipper's access in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions).

Changes

This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version.
