The terms in five parts

What Clipper does

Clipper makes short clips from videos and, when you approve a clip, sends it to the accounts you have connected. On TikTok, clips are sent to your drafts; nothing is published until you publish it yourself in the TikTok app.

Your responsibilities with Clipper

  • Only clip and post videos you own or have permission to use.
  • Follow the terms and community guidelines of each platform you post to, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Review every clip, its title and its caption before you post it.

Connected accounts in Clipper

You can disconnect an account at any time in Clipper, and remove Clipper's access in that platform's own settings.
